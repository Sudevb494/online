Monstera Deliciosa

The Monstera Deliciosa is one of the most popular indoor plants. It has beautiful split leaves and grows well in bright indirect light.

General Characteristics
  • It is a climbing evergreen vine species.
  • Features large, glossy heart-shaped leaves.
  • Develops unique natural splits and holes (fenestrations).
  • Uses aerial roots to climb upward on trees or poles.
  • Produces edible fruit in its native tropical wild habitat.

Repotting Recommendation

When to Repot?

Repot your Monstera every 1–2 years or when roots start growing out of the drainage holes.

  • ✔ Use fresh potting mix
  • ✔ Choose a pot 2 inches larger
  • ✔ Water after repotting
  • ✔ Keep in indirect sunlight

Plant Parent Tips

💧 Water Carefully

Avoid overwatering. Let the top soil dry before watering again.

☀ Bright Light

Place near a bright window but avoid direct afternoon sunlight.

🍃 Clean Leaves

Wipe leaves gently with a damp cloth to remove dust.
